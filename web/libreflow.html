Your data

Import the Polar export ZIP or (faster for large exports) the unzipped folder, the trainingen_json folder from polar_ble.py, or a backup. Every import is added to what is already there (duplicates are skipped) and stored only on this device.

…or drop the files here.

A backup is a ZIP with everything (summary, per-second data, routes, shoes, plan and zones); the iPhone app imports it too.

How do I get my full Polar history?
  1. Sign in at account.polar.com (the same account as Polar Flow).
  2. Choose “Download your data” and request the export.
  3. You get an e-mail with a download link to a ZIP containing training-session-*.json files.
  4. Load that ZIP (or the unzipped folder) here. The iPhone app imports the same ZIP (Settings › Your data › Import file…).

The watch itself keeps only its last few trainings, so the export is the way to get your full history.

    Km per year

    Seasonal pattern

    Average km per calendar month over all years.

    Pace trend

    Average pace per run (min/km) and monthly median.

    Aerobic efficiency

    Metres per heartbeat (speed ÷ heart rate), monthly median. Higher = fitter.

    Fitness (VDOT estimate)

    Best VDOT per quarter, from whole runs ≥ 3 km and best efforts.

    Intensity distribution

    Training time per month by heart rate zone (last 12 months).

    Deeper analysis

    Fitness, fatigue and form

    Training load (TRIMP from your per-second heart rate, all sports). Fitness = 6-week average, fatigue = 1-week average, form = the difference.

    Best efforts within runs

    Fastest 1 km, 5 km and 10 km anywhere within a run, best per quarter (min/km).

    Aerobic decoupling

    How much your efficiency (speed per heartbeat) drops in the second half, activities ≥ 40 min. Below 5% = strong aerobic base.

    Polar running index

    Polar's own fitness estimate per run, monthly median.

    Cadence

    Steps per minute, monthly median.

    Personal records within runs

    From the per-second distance data, so a fast 5 km in the middle of a long run counts too.
