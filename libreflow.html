How do I get my full Polar history?
  1. Sign in at account.polar.com (the same account as Polar Flow).
  2. Choose “Download your data” and request the export.
  3. You get an e-mail with a download link to a ZIP containing training-session-*.json files.
  4. Load that ZIP (or the unzipped folder) here. The iPhone app can import the same ZIP directly (Settings › Import file…).

The Polar AccessLink API only returns trainings from the moment you connect; the export is the way to get your full history.
